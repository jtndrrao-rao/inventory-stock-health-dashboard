<h2 align="center">🔄 The Old Way vs This Way</h2>

<div align="center">

| 😩 The old way | ✅ With StockPulse Inventory Dashboard |
|:---|:---|
| 😩 Stock lists with no priorities | ✅ ABC analysis shows the few SKUs that drive 80% of revenue |
| 😩 Stock-outs found too late | ✅ Reorder alerts ranked by days of cover, with a suggested order quantity |
| 😩 Cash stuck in slow stock | ✅ Cash Locked and Dead Stock values, with the biggest cash traps listed |
| 😩 Targets nobody tracks | ✅ Stock turns, actual against target, by category |
| 😩 Fixed formulas you cannot change | ✅ Fields & Formulas and Rules & Targets pages to tune the logic |

<h2 align="center">✨ What You Get</h2>

<table>
<tr>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

#### 📈 ABC Analysis
A, B and C classes with a Pareto curve, revenue share, stock share and the control level each class needs.

</td>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

#### 🩺 Stock Health
Every SKU tagged Fast, Healthy, Slow, Dead or Out of Stock, with stock ageing by days since last sale.

</td>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

#### 🔔 Reorder Alerts
Critical and reorder items sorted by urgency with days of cover and a suggested order quantity.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

#### 🔒 Cash Locked
Dead stock and excess slow stock valued in rupees, with the sales at risk if critical SKUs are not refilled.

</td>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

#### 🖱️ Click to Filter
Click any bar, slice, chip or row to filter the whole dashboard. Hold Ctrl to pick several.

</td>
<td width="33%" valign="top">

#### ⚙️ Your Rules
Tune formulas, targets and thresholds, load your own stock file, and reset to the sample at any time.

</td>
</tr>
</table>
